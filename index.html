Common questions

Frequently asked.

What is depth-oriented therapy?
It works with the underlying patterns, beliefs and relationship dynamics that drive symptoms, aiming for lasting change rather than short-term relief. It is combined with practical skills where useful.
Do you offer online therapy across India?
Yes. Sessions are held over secure video and are available to clients anywhere in India.
What issues do you work with?
Anxiety, OCD, trauma, depression, personality patterns, body image, relationship difficulties and couples concerns.
How do I book a session?
Use the form below or message on WhatsApp. Sessions run Tuesday to Sunday, 9:30am – 5:30pm IST. Mondays closed.
I'm not sure therapy is for me. Can I still reach out?
Yes. Many people start unsure. A short message or first conversation is a good way to ask questions and decide, with no obligation to continue.
What happens in the first session?
We talk about what brings you in, your background and what you'd like to change. You can share at your own pace. By the end, we'll discuss whether working together makes sense and what that would look like.
What does a session cost?
Fees are shared when you get in touch, along with available times.
Is therapy confidential?
Yes, within standard legal and ethical limits relating to serious risk of harm, which are explained in the first session.
