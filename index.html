The approach

Not a quick fix.
A real understanding.

Many people arrive after trying tips, apps and short courses that helped for a while. Depth work asks why the same feelings return, and changes things at that level.

i.

Trauma-informed

Safety comes first. We work at the edge of what feels manageable, building steadiness in the body before going near what hurts.

ii.

Psychodynamic

We look at how early relationships shape the way you love, work and protect yourself today, so old patterns lose their grip.

iii.

Practical, too

Alongside depth, you leave with tools: grounding, cognitive reframing and emotion regulation you can use between sessions.

Common questions

Things people often ask.

Still unsure? Send a message. I'm happy to answer.

What is depth-oriented therapy?
It looks beyond symptoms to the feelings, memories and relationship patterns underneath them. The aim is lasting change rather than short-term relief, though we work on day-to-day coping too.
Is online therapy as effective as in-person?
For most people, yes. Research shows comparable outcomes for many concerns. Sessions run on a secure video platform, and all you need is a private space and a stable connection.
How long will therapy take?
It varies. Some people come for a few months for a specific concern; deeper work often takes longer. We review progress together regularly, and you're always free to pause or stop.
Is what I share confidential?
Yes. Everything is confidential, with the standard legal and ethical exceptions around serious risk of harm, which I'll explain clearly in our first session.
